GUIDE/What's new

What's new

What has changed in what you see in the program, newest first. A line is added with every update that touches what the user sees. “Preview” means it has been built but its testing in Civil 3D is not yet complete.

2026-11-19

Section titled “2026-11-19”
  • Fix rBProfileInvert (preview): in the Boru akarı gir window, choosing the start or end manhole from the list used to close Civil 3D (it showed when choosing the same manhole, but every choice closed it). Now the chosen manhole sets the profile and the window stays open.

2026-11-18

Section titled “2026-11-18”
  • Selecting from the profile rBSelect, rBProfileInvert (preview): the selection button’s methods Borular, Bacalar, Bacadan bacaya, Kol and Hat now work in the drawn longitudinal profiles too — in every window, in the Boykesit tab, and in the Boru akarı gir window: when the cursor gets near a manhole’s column or a pipe’s wall it lights up yellow, so you do not have to click exactly on it, and what is selected turns yellow in the plan and in every profile it appears in. Before, a profile could only be selected from with CAD seçimi and a right click, by clicking exactly on the line.

2026-11-17

Section titled “2026-11-17”
  • One entry in the right-click menu rBProfile (preview): our tools are no longer added one by one to the right-click menu in Civil 3D; there is now a single entry, UrBIM 3D ▸ — like the Display Order ▸ entry in AutoCAD — and the tools are in its side menu in the same order. Each works as it did.
  • One update after rBRefresh rBRefresh (preview): when it applies something, the labels, profiles and tables are all updated once — they used to be all updated a second time in the next command. And if you cancel the preview, or there was nothing to apply, only what changed is looked at.
  • Check and profiles in drawings with many networks (preview): rBCheck, the Sağlık tab and the recalculation of all the profiles now read the drawing’s manholes and pipes once, not once per network.
  • Selecting and clicking in drawings with many networks (preview): the proximity highlight when snapping, clicking on the rows of tables and panels, and rBInspect now open only the network’s own manholes and pipes from the drawing index, not the manholes of all the networks for every network; so in a drawing with dozens of networks they are shorter by that factor.
  • One pass in drawings with many networks (preview): rBRefresh, rBStatus, the Sağlık tab, rBCheck and rBControl now go through the drawing once for all the networks, not once per network; so in a drawing with dozens of networks this pass is shorter by that factor. What they find does not change.
  • Fewer passes over the whole drawing (preview): the edit commands (such as rBMove), clicking on the rows of tables and panels, rBInspect, and the proximity highlight when snapping now find the network’s manholes and pipes from the drawing index, not by going through the whole drawing. This is governed by Çizim dizini too. rBRefresh and rBCheck still go through the whole drawing.
  • Profiles with one read (preview): when all the profiles are recalculated — after an undo (U), in rBRefresh, and when the slope format or the label templates change — the drawing is read once, not once per profile. Most of this calculation was repeating the same reading, so it is much shorter now.
  • The quick update is more precise rBSettings (preview): adding, deleting or reversing a pipe no longer recalculates all the network’s profiles, only those whose path changed and those near the edit. And saving the drawing commands’ settings file does not recalculate everything; all of them are looked at only when the slope format alone changes.

2026-11-16

Section titled “2026-11-16”
  • Faster profile state rBSettings (preview): the Boykesit, Sağlık and Başla tabs and the profile editing commands (rBProfileHelper, rBProfileMove, rBProfileCell…) recalculate, after an edit, the state of only the profiles the edit touched — moving a manhole recalculates the state of only the profile that shows it, out of 33. This is governed by Hızlı güncelleme too: Kapalı calculates them all as before.
  • The quick update rBSettings (preview): after an edit, only what the edit touched among the labels, profiles and tables can be calculated — moving a manhole recalculates only the profile that shows it, out of 33. The new setting Hızlı güncelleme: Açık (the default: only what was touched, fast — the update after rBMove takes about 0.15 s instead of 1.7 s), Doğrula (what was touched first, then everything; the difference is written to the log and fixed, and there is no speed-up — for when you are looking into a problem), Kapalı (as it was). The result is the same in the three: nothing is redrawn unless what it shows has changed.
  • Accumulated load in the table rBTable, rBNetworks (preview): fields for pipe templates with the load the pipe has collected from the start of the network — the population, the residential and industrial areas, the household and industrial averages, «Ek debi», the area F, ΣF·C, and the weighted C — and Hidrolik durum: «Tamam», «Eksik: …» or «Hesaplanamadı: …». And Hidrolik varsayılanları: the consumption, the industrial discharge unit and the inlet time when a pipe or a catchment leaves them empty.
  • Catchments in the table rBTable (preview): two fields for templates — Havza sayısı and Havza alanı: the catchments of each pipe and manhole, read from the drawing whenever the table is filled. In this table only; not in the label nor in the drawn table.
  • Selecting from the table no longer leaves an empty U step (preview): clicking a row in Sistem Tablosu, Göster in the Boykesit tab, and double-clicking a row in Gezgin, Sağlık and Metraj, no longer add an empty step to the undo list: the first U after the click brings back the last real edit (and the view before it, if you zoomed in). And opening the table and Yeniden tara do not clear the drawing’s selection; and when you select something in the drawing the table does not select it again, nor does it zoom the view by itself while Yakınlaştır is on.
  • The catchment in «Özellikler» rBProperties, rBCheck (preview): when only catchments are selected, the panel writes their name, their use (İskan / Sanayi) and their hydraulic inputs, and shows their area and what they are linked to; and a single pipe or manhole lists the catchments that drain into it, with their areas. And rBCheck names the catchments that are unlinked and those that have no area.
  • The «Havza alanı» catchments rBCatchment (preview): closed polylines become catchments that are linked to the pipe or manhole their water drains into, and their area is read live from the drawing. And when a manhole is removed and its two pipes merge, their catchments move to the pipe that remains; and a copied catchment gets its own identity in rBRefresh and comes unlinked. The catchment’s own information comes in the next step to the Özellikler panel.
  • Hydraulic inputs in the table rBTable (preview): the hydraulic inputs are fields for labels and tables; and when they are added to a template they are written into the cell — the load of many pipes at once. They can be shown in labels too.
  • Hydraulic inputs rBProperties (preview): the Hidrolik girdiler section saves the numbers that the hydraulic calculation will take from the pipe and the manhole — in sewer pipes İskan alanı, Sanayi alanı, Nüfus yoğunluğu, Nüfus, Kullanma suyu and Sanayi birim debisi; in storm-water pipes Kısmi alan, Akış katsayısı C and Giriş süresi; and in manholes Ek debi. With several elements selected, the shared value appears and what you write reaches all of them. The calculation itself has not been built yet.

2026-11-15

Section titled “2026-11-15”
  • U and REDO are right with the windows open: if you selected a pipe and then deleted it with ERASE, the first U used to bring back nothing; now it brings it back. And with Çalışma Alanı, Özellikler or Sistem Tablosu open, REDO after U used to do nothing; now it brings back what you undid.
  • The calculation method rBStandards (preview): a standard can now carry how its networks are calculated — in the new Hesap yöntemi tab each step has a method (roughness: k or n according to the material; full flow: Colebrook-White or Manning; the partial-flow table), and its coefficients and tables. The tables are taken from Excel or PDF with Yapıştır. The hydraulic calculation itself has not been built yet, and the built-in İSKİ method has not been written yet.
  • The general limit and the exception limit rBStandards, rBControl (preview): a condition can now carry a second limit that the document allows as an exception (two new columns, İstisna değeri and İstisna a (mm)). If the value exceeds the general limit but stays within the exception, the violation is informational, and if it exceeds it too, it is an error. In the built-in İSKİ: sewer backfill 2.70 m and the exception 1.50 m (5.1.3); drop 4.5 m and the exception 7 m (7.1.10); slope 1/(Ø − 100) and the exception 1/Ø (5.1.7).

2026-11-14

Section titled “2026-11-14”
  • The drawing index rBSettings (preview): an index instead of scanning the whole drawing every time to find the labels, profiles, manholes and pipes. The default is Açık: updating the profiles after moving a manhole became about half as long. And Doğrula compares the index with the scan, and the result does not change.

2026-11-13

Section titled “2026-11-13”
  • «Last used» works rBSelect (preview): Son seçilen in the selection button now finds what the previous command worked on; it used to always say Bu çizimde henüz bir seçim kullanılmadı..
  • The Çalışma Alanı tabs see the windows’ edits (preview): after an edit from a window such as Sistem Tablosu, the tab you open is read afresh; it used to show the previous values until the next command.
  • Types in Excel rBCatalogs (preview): Excel’e aktar… writes the pipe and node types to an Excel file — a sheet for each kind, a row for each type, and a column for each type parameter; and Excel’den al… reads the edited file: a changed value changes its type, a new row is a new type, and no type is deleted. The result appears in the window and Kaydet checks it like any edit; and what was not taken is listed in the bottom line with its reasons.
  • Each item from its library rBCatalogUpdate (preview): the drawing saves which library each type and Poz item came from; and Update from the library compares each item with that library — if it is on this computer — and takes it from there, and groups the items in the window by their libraries. The libraries that are not on the computer are named by the command line, and what came from them is compared with the active one.
  • Multiple libraries rBLibraries, rBCatalogs (preview): name your libraries — «Şirket standardı», «Üretici X», «İSKİ»… — and choose the active one; a new one is a copy of the active one or has the starter catalog, and a shared folder on the network can be added. A library is exported as a single .urbimlib file and imported on another computer as a new library. And Katalog Yönetimi shows the name of the library it is editing.
  • Taking from another drawing rBCatalogTransfer (preview): another drawing’s catalog is read from its file; its items that are not here are shown ticked, and the different ones unticked with their differences; and what you choose is taken with what it needs into this drawing or into your library.
  • Deleting what is unused rBCatalogPurge (preview): the items of the drawing’s copy of the catalog that no pipe, manhole or network uses — types, accessories, parameters, materials, trench types, fitting families and Poz items — are shown all ticked; and what you untick stays with what it needs. Nothing that is in use is shown.
  • A built-in İSKİ standard rBStandards (preview): the conditions of İSKİ’s sewer and storm-water specifications, each with its article number — minimum slope 1/(Ø − 100) and not less than 1/1000; Ø300; backfill 2.70 m / 1.20 m; between the two manholes 70 / 120 / 150 m; a drop manhole from 0.60 m, and a maximum drop of 4.5 m. A condition can now be written as a formula in the diameter too; and the limits of fullness, velocity and water depth are kept for the hydraulic calculation. And in rBControl there is a new violation: «Düşülü baca».

2026-11-12

Section titled “2026-11-12”
  • Parameters in labels and tables rBLabelStyle, rBTable (preview): the two new fields Parametre (for a manhole) and Boru parametresi (for a pipe) read a parameter by its name — the element’s own value or its type’s value; and in a table column the names are chosen from a list.
  • The element’s parameters in Özellikler rBProperties (preview): your parameters at the «Öğe» level appear as boxes for the selected pipes and manholes — with the shared value; and what is written or chosen is written by «Uygula» into every selected element. For a single element, the values of its type’s parameters appear in the information section.
  • Your own parameters rBCatalogs (preview): in the new Parametreler tab you define fields such as the manufacturer, the year of construction and the tightness test — the kind (number, text, yes / no, list), the unit, the options, which elements it is for, and the level (type or element). And the values of type parameters are written under the pipe sizes and the manhole types; and under a manhole type, Ekler, Kaplamalar and Parametreler became tabs.
  • One manhole’s coatings rBCoatings, rBProperties (preview): make one manhole’s coatings differ from its type’s coatings — another material, face, thickness or extent, or removing or adding one; the manhole saves only what differs from its type, so changing the type later reaches what it did not change. And Özellikler shows the manhole’s coatings.
  • Manhole coatings («Kaplamalar») rBCatalogs (preview): under each manhole type, beside Ekler, the new Kaplamalar section — bituminous insulation on the outer face, epoxy on the inner…: the material, the face, the thickness or the number of layers, and the extent (the whole height, from the bottom, from the top). And the materials tab is now called Malzemeler (it was Dolgu malzemeleri); a material that a coating uses cannot be deleted, and it comes with the type into the drawing. Data for now: it does not enter the quantities.
  • Manhole accessories rBAccessories (preview): make one manhole’s accessories differ from its type’s accessories — another quantity, or removing, or adding from the library; the manhole saves only what differs, and changing the type reaches the rows it did not change. And the accessories appear under the manhole in Özellikler.
  • Accessories rBCatalogs (preview): in the new Ekler tab you define accessories such as ladders, gaskets and sleeves (name, unit, Poz, description); and each manhole type names them with a quantity in the Ekler section under it. Data for now: it does not enter the quantities.
  • The manhole type’s stack and its preview rBCatalogs (preview): the depth tiers and the part limits (the shaft, the cone, the adjusting rings and the cover — the least and the most) of a type whose base has been chosen are edited under the type; and Önizle shows the parts the engine stacks at a trial depth.
  • Manhole parts are edited in UrBIM rBCatalogs (preview): a new tab, Baca parçaları — the parts of the ready-made manhole families (base, rings, cone, adjusting rings, cover) are added, edited and ordered by their height, opening, wall, volumes and Poz; and a part that is in use cannot be deleted. And İçe aktar… takes the families from UrbanoMetraj’s parts file.
  • A check warning before the take-off rBMetraj (preview): if there are open check errors in the networks that will be calculated, the command line gives their number and points to rBControl; accepted ones are not counted. And the take-off is calculated all the same.
  • The manhole type names its base rBCatalogs (preview): a new column Taban in the manhole types — when a base from the type’s family of its size is chosen, its manholes are stacked on it, and the connection bases are not chosen. And Kurallardan türet adds a type for every base the rules give (Ø1000 / Ø200, Ø1000 / Ø300–400…). And the types whose base was not chosen are calculated as they were.
  • The check report rBControl (preview): «Excel raporu» writes the whole check beside the drawing and opens it — the summary, the violations (the accepted ones green with their reasons), and the pipes and manholes (backfill, depth, slope, the number of open violations).
  • Accepting violations and marking them in the drawing rBControl (preview): the selected violations are accepted with a reason — they show grey and are not counted as an error, they are saved in the drawing and stay in the following checks, and they can be undone. And «Çizime işaretle» marks the violations shown with circles and names on a layer that does not print, and «İşaretleri sil» removes them.
  • The bill of quantities reads the item book rBMetrajKesif, rBMetraj (preview): a row that has no description is described by the item book; and with Keşif açıklaması Poz kitabından in the Metraj tab the book takes precedence in every row. And if the item is paid in another unit, a note appears on the unit cell — with no conversion.
  • The item book and the materials library rBCatalogs, rBCatalogUpdate (preview): a new tab, Poz kitabı — for each Poz number its description, its unit and its source (with no price); it is gathered the first time from the numbers in your catalogs, and Kataloglardan ekle adds what is missing. And in the Malzemeler tab a material now has a kind, a unit and a density. And the drawing carries a copy of the items it uses, and Kütüphaneden güncelle compares them too.
  • Checking the network rBControl (preview): the basic checks and the design checks in one window. The design checks test, against the network’s standard, the slope, the backfill, the depth, the pipe length, the diameter, the drop in a manhole, the reduction of the diameter, the manhole depth and the entry angle, and every violation names the standard and its article. They are filtered by severity, by network and by the elements selected beforehand, and «Göster» goes to them. And it does not stop any calculation.
  • Manhole parts by hand rBManholeStack (preview): replace a ready-made manhole’s rings, cone, adjusting rings and cover, or add, delete or order them; the window draws the manhole to scale and does not save until the height reaches its depth («Motora bırak» goes back to the engine’s suggestion). And rBMetraj afterwards calculates with these parts.
  • Manhole parts in the manhole itself rBMetraj, rBProperties (preview): the take-off writes each manhole’s parts into the manhole itself; and when a single manhole is selected, «Baca parçaları» appears in Özellikler — their source, the parts from the bottom with their heights, and their total and the depth. Editing by hand is in the next step.
  • Design standards rBStandards (preview): the conditions of the design check — minimum and maximum slope, backfill, depth, the length between two manholes, the smallest diameter, the drop… The built-in standards are read-only, and you write, copy and edit your own. The slope is written in the settings’ format, and conflicting conditions are reported at once. And each network chooses its standard in the new «Standart» column in rBNetworks, and it is copied into the drawing.
  • «Durdur» (preview): in the wait window, for long commands that write their work all at once (rBProfile, rBProfileUpdate, rBLabelAdd, rBLabelUpdate, rBLabelClash, rBConvert, rBSelfTest), a «Durdur» button stops the command and the drawing stays as it was before it.
  • Stages and a counter in the wait window (preview): the profiles, the labels, rBSelfTest, rBConvert and rBGround count what has finished («12 / 34»), and rBMetraj, rBRefresh and rBCrossSection write the current stage. And rBLabelClash shows its progress in this window instead of AutoCAD’s status bar.
  • A wait window in long jobs (preview): when a command is processing the drawing and the program does not respond for more than a second, a small window appears: the command’s name, the current stage, a moving bar, and the elapsed time. It does not appear while the command is waiting for a click or a selection, nor while a window is open. The delay and enabling the window are in rBSettings in the section «Bekleme penceresi».
  • Updating from the library rBCatalogUpdate (preview): it compares the catalog items the drawing carries — the products, the excavation and the fitting — with the library on your computer, and shows what differs field by field; and it takes what you choose as it is in the library. So the value you correct in the library can now reach a drawing that is in progress. And the manhole symbols, labels, tables and longitudinal profiles are updated in the same step, and the quantities become stale.
  • Moving between tabs is faster rBWorkspace (preview): the tab is not read again if nothing in the drawing has changed, so it appears at once; and if something has changed, it appears at once with its previous content and «Güncelleniyor…» instead of the grey pane. And the states of the profiles and the take-off are calculated once per change and are shared by «Başla», «Boykesit», «Sağlık» and «Metraj».

2026-11-11

Section titled “2026-11-11”
  • Marking several rows in the tables rBProfiles (preview): in the «Boykesit» tab and in rBHat, rBNetworks, rBSelect, rBCatalogs, rBLevels and rBCheck, several rows are marked with Ctrl or Shift, and the button deletes them together, or shows them, or accepts them. And in «Boykesit» a new «Güncelle» button updates only the marked profiles.
  • The selection icons rBLabel (preview): as in Urbano, under «Seçim» are the icons for labelling, updating, resolving overlaps and deleting; each works only on the selection’s labels of the panel’s kind, and the selection stays for what comes after. And «Tıklayarak etiketle» and «Etiket taşı» are independent of the selection, by clicking.
  • The selection button knows what the command works on rBSelect (preview): each window shows only the methods useful to it — no «Bacalar» in the profile or in naming pipes — and its list shows what the command works on: manholes for manhole labels, and lines for the profile. And «Hat», «Kol» and «Bacadan bacaya» select several until Enter, and a second click on a selected line takes it out.
  • Linear interpolation for several lines rBGround (preview): each selected line is filled between the values of its two end manholes.
  • The flow arrow’s place and colour rBLabelStyle (preview): the arrow after the texts, or in the middle of the pipe, or at a distance from one of its ends; with an offset along the pipe, an offset above or below the pipe, and a colour of its own.
  • The flow arrow as a block rBLabelStyle (preview): in the pipe, line and branch templates, «Akış oku» is now drawn as lines or as a block — your own block from the drawing or UrBIM’s filled arrow — pointing in the pipe’s flow direction.
  • Profile template: the ready-made manhole’s parts in the drawn profiles rBProfileStyle (preview): by turning off «Prefabrik bacaların basitleştirilmiş çizimi», the longitudinal profile draws each ready-made manhole its parts as the last rBMetraj calculated them, the top one at the cover; and a new calculation redraws the profile.
  • Fix: the profile template window rBProfileStyle: changing «Veri» of a row (especially a new row) used to freeze the window and then close AutoCAD. Now every choice is applied once.
  • The list of what was selected, before running rBSelect (preview): in every command that has a selection, beside what was selected there is ⌕ to zoom in (and double-clicking), ✖ to take rows out of the selection, and ✔ to keep the marked ones alone — as in Urbano 11. And the windows that have no list of elements (rBClear, rBSelect, the «Etiketler» tab…) now have, under the selection button, a list of what was selected.
  • «Boykesit çiz» rBProfile (preview): «Yeni boykesit…» from the tab shows the profiles it will draw before it asks for the point — the name, the two manholes, the number of pipes and the length — so you can zoom in to one of them, or take some out, or keep only some, then «Çiz».
  • The selection button everywhere rBLabel rBProfiles rBHat (preview): «Seçim» in the «Etiketler» and «Boykesit» tabs, and «Elle hat ekle» in the lines window, became the same selection button that is in the windows — the method’s icon, and ▾ with the methods and their icons, and choosing a method starts the selection at once — instead of the list of words and the «Bacadan bacaya ekle» button. And in the lines window a whole branch can now be added as a line.
  • Fix: the panels follow the active drawing rBWorkspace (preview): after opening a new drawing or switching to another one, «Başla», «Sistemler», «Boykesit» and the properties panel used to stay on the previous drawing. Now they all show the drawing in front of you, and the new drawing appears empty.

2026-11-10
