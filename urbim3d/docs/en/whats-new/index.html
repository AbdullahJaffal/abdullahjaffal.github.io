GUIDE/What's new

What's new

What has changed in what you see in the program, newest first. A line is added with every update that touches what the user sees. “Preview” means it has been built but its testing in Civil 3D is not yet complete.

2026-11-04

Section titled “2026-11-04”
  • Cover height and depth below the pipe in rBNodeType (preview): with checkboxes, as in Urbano — the type, the cover, the depth, or any of them, for many manholes at once; the depth is measured from Borunun dış kenarının altından or from the invert level. The depth can also be edited in the properties panel, and cleared with rBClear.
  • Ribbon editor (rBRibbon, in the Tanımlar panel) (preview): as in Urbano Tools — the panels and their order, each command large / small / in the ▾ menu / hidden, families that group commands into one drop-down button, drag and drop, and export and import. The ribbon changes while the window is open, and the layout is saved on this computer. rBPipe, rBNode and rBNetwork, which are not in the ribbon, can now be put on a button.
  • Windows on every screen (preview): on a laptop at 125 % or 150 % scaling, and on 2K and 4K screens, every window opens fully inside the screen with its bottom buttons visible; if the screen is smaller than the window itself, its content scrolls instead of the text being cut off. Settings scrolls, with its buttons fixed at the bottom. Ribbon icons are now sharp at high scaling instead of blurred.
  • BricsCAD V26 edition (preview): the same program with its commands, windows and ribbon, loaded in BricsCAD V26 with NETLOAD. Its surfaces are BricsCAD’s own TIN surfaces: «Yüzeyden» in rBGround, and drawing, read the levels and break points from them, as in Civil 3D.
  • Separate commands for pipe type and manhole type — rBPipeType and rBNodeType (preview): the counterparts of Urbano’s «Çap - Malzeme» and «Baca verisi ayarla» windows. Select the elements with the selection button, then the group and the type (or Çizimden al), and you see the type’s full data; every element is in the list with its current and new type, what will change is yellow, and nothing is written before Uygula. Step through one by one with the arrows with Otomatik yaklaş, and the window stays open after applying. Changing a manhole’s type changes its symbol in the drawing at once — from the properties panel and the table too. The Çap bilgisi and Baca tipi stages in «Başla» now open these two commands.
  • The user guide fully reviewed against the current program: Tutorial 5 — Names and labels is new, Tutorial 1 starts from the workspace, and the «Etiketler» panel and its commands are in the interface and the command index.
  • Clearing data (rBClear) (preview): a new command in the «Kotlar» panel that clears what you tick — pipe type, the two invert levels, break points, manhole type, level values (for one level or for one source such as «Yüzeyden»), cover height — from the network, a line or a selection, while the pipes and manholes stay. A preview with counts before clearing, and undo in a single step.
  • Table templates window (rBTable → «Şablonlar…») (preview): a new template for manholes or for pipes, or a copy of a template, with its columns from the field tree — their order, showing and hiding them, their heading, their number of decimal places, and the level or section point — and every save shows in the table at once.
  • Arrange the «Başla» stages yourself in rBWorkspace (preview): the Düzenle button shows, hides and moves the stages with ▲▼; the next one is the first incomplete stage in your order, and the order is saved for each drawing. New stages you can choose: Çap bilgisi, Baca tipi, Hatlar, Baca etiketleri and Boru etiketleri.
  • The drawing scale is now optional in the labels panel (preview): a «Çizim ölçeği» checkbox — unticked, templates are drawn at their own values; ticked, the scale field appears. Drawings that have a saved scale stay as they are.
  • Network table templates (rBTable) (preview): a «Şablon» list — the general view as before, or a template for manholes or for pipes with columns from the label fields, with their decimal places and headings (two ready-made templates on Urbano’s columns). The same selection button as in the windows limits the table to a line, a branch or a saved set, and «Tümü» brings back everything; «Hatlara göre sırala»; zooming in the drawing when a row is selected (centre, or zoom by a factor); and the totals row «Boru: n (m) / N (m)». The selection button replaces «Yalnız seçililer».
  • A «Seçim» list in the labels panel (rBLabel) (preview): as in Urbano — label the whole network, or a line, a branch or what lies between two manholes, a saved selection, the last selection, or what you select; the button is now «Etiketle».
  • Two new pipe fields: «Arazi başlangıç» and «Arazi bitiş» (preview): the ground level at the start manhole and at the end manhole, in the field tree in rBLabelStyle — by the network’s depth level or by a level you name.

2026-11-03

Section titled “2026-11-03”
  • Labelling by clicking (rBLabelPick) (preview): a «Tıklayarak etiketle» button in the labels panel — click a manhole, a pipe or a pipe of a line and it is labelled at once with the panel’s template and position, then the next one, until Enter.
  • Label position from the panel (rBLabel) (preview): «Konum» with three choices, as in Urbano — the label stays where it is (and changing the template keeps the label’s position), or it is put back in the template’s position, or a leader line with one of the arrows ↖ ↗ ↙ ↘ and an angle from the horizontal.
  • A simpler labels panel (rBLabel) (preview): one kind at a time (Bacalar / Borular / Hatlar / Kollar) with only its templates below it; the network’s template first, marked with a star ★; the ★ button makes the selected one the network’s template, and the ✎ button opens the template in the template manager; and a single «Seçerek etiketle» button for the selected kind.
  • Fixed pipe length in rBConvert (preview): tick «Sabit boru uzunluğu» and type the pipe length (6 m, for example), and only pieces whose length is a multiple of it — within the merge tolerance — become pipes; every other piece stays an ordinary line as it is, and a purple band shows them to you before the conversion.
  • The Ağlar tab in the colours of the other tabs (preview): it was dark, following the AutoCAD theme; it is now light like the rest of the workspace, so it no longer looks like a foreign window inside it.
  • Workspace (rBWorkspace) (preview): one window docked on the right instead of six separate panels — Ağlar, Çizim, Etiketler, Gezgin and Sağlık are tabs in it, and each of their commands opens its tab. Its first tab is Başla: choosing the network, the work stages (drawing, checking, ground level, invert level) with their counters, the next step highlighted with a button that does it, and a search for any command with no need for Turkish characters. Properties and the network table stay as two separate windows.
  • One window for conversion in rBConvert (preview): you select the lines by layer — each layer with its element count and length — or from the drawing, and you choose the network, the pipe and manhole types and the merge tolerance in the same window; the counts and the red rings change with every tick and every number. An element that is already in a network is not converted a second time.
  • Explanations in «?» instead of lines under the fields (preview): in every window and panel, a field’s explanation is now in a small «?» circle next to its name — hover the cursor over it to show it, or click it to keep it open until you click elsewhere (or Esc). The windows are much shorter, and messages, warnings and values (such as the selected pipe diameter in rBDraw) are still visible.
  • Preview of the result while selecting in rBSelect (preview): in Hat and Kol the whole line or branch is highlighted when you approach a pipe, and in Bacadan bacaya the whole route from the first manhole to the manhole you are approaching. Highlighted pipes are now a wide band, not a thin line.

2026-11-02

Section titled “2026-11-02”
  • Yellow highlight on approach (preview): when you select a manhole or a pipe by clicking in rBSelect (manhole to manhole, branch, line — and in every window that has the selection button), the element is highlighted in yellow as soon as the cursor comes near it, the click selects it, and the selected element stays yellow. The distance is a new setting, «Yaklaşma mesafesi», in rBSettings, as in Urbano.
  • Selecting pipes or manholes by clicking in rBSelect (preview): each click on what is highlighted in yellow adds it or removes it, and clicking in empty space starts a rectangle — to the right a window, to the left a crossing — and the selected elements stay yellow; Enter finishes.
  • Highlighting while drawing (preview): in rBNetwork and rBPipe the manhole the click will connect to is highlighted with a yellow ring — by the same «Yakalama mesafesi» rule — and in rBNode and rBReconnect the pipe is highlighted when you approach it and is selected by clicking next to it.

2026-11-01

Section titled “2026-11-01”
  • Label templates (rBLabelStyle) (preview): creating, editing, copying and deleting templates; lines are built from field “chips”, and every change shows in the preview as it will in the drawing; XML export and import; and «Çizimdeki kopyayı güncelle» redraws this drawing’s labels with the new template. «Şablonlar» in the ribbon, and «Şablonları düzenle…» in the panel.
  • Fix: a template written in paper millimetres did not draw a label at a 1:1000 scale; it now does.
  • Labels panel (rBLabel) (preview) and the «Etiketler» panel in the ribbon: the template for each kind, each network’s template, the drawing scale, and the label commands as buttons; and «Çakışma Çöz» in the ribbon.
  • Label template library in %AppData%\UrBIM3D\LabelTemplates\library.json, with a template for each network and each label kind, and a drawing scale for templates whose sizes are in paper millimetres — and templates are exported and imported with an XML file. (Choosing them from a window comes with the labels panel.)
  • Line and branch labels (preview): rBLabelAdd → «Hat» or «Kol» → pipes: a label for each line (its name and length) or branch (“from–to” and its length) in the middle of its route, which updates with it and goes when it goes.
  • Pipe labels (preview): rBLabelAdd accepts pipes — length and slope above the pipe, diameter and family below it; when crowded, it slides along the pipe, then flips side, then moves out with a leader line, and a short pipe gets its label outside it. The label follows its pipe, and deleting, moving and overlap resolution work with it.
  • Labels do not overlap one another (preview): rBLabelAdd always places each label in a free spot — it turns around its manhole, then moves away up to twice its distance — and rBLabelClash resolves overlaps between existing labels and leaves the free ones where they are.
  • Levels of incoming pipes at the manhole (preview): a pipe that enters the manhole at a level different from the outgoing one has its level written on the pipe itself, and its end is marked with a triangle pointing to the manhole — as in UrbanoLabels. rBLabelAdd
  • Moving labels (rBLabelMove) (preview): move a label with two clicks and its leader line is reconnected to the manhole at once, label after label, until Enter.
  • rBRenumber retired: it is replaced by rBName — «Adlandır (otomatik)» starts from the network’s template and so does what it used to do, together with the other modes.
  • Labels follow the model (preview): every one of our commands, and every edit from the properties panel or the table, ends by updating the manhole labels — a moved manhole takes its label with it, a new level shows at once, and a deleted manhole takes its label with it — and a label you dragged by hand stays where you put it. After ordinary AutoCAD edits: rBLabelUpdate or rBRefresh.
  • Invert level while drawing (preview): a fixed depth below a level, at a point of the pipe section you choose, in the drawing panel — the same method as rBInvert, for each pipe the moment it is drawn.
  • Ground levels from the surface while drawing (preview): choose a surface in the drawing panel, and every manhole you draw takes its level and every pipe its break points, by the same rules as rBGround. Civil 3D only.
  • Renaming (rBName) (preview): manholes, pipes or lines with a prefix, a template, a suffix and a counter with a step, in the current order, line by line, from the farthest manhole to the outfall, or by coordinates, with a preview before writing.
  • A bend without a manhole while drawing (preview): type Kirik during rBNetwork and the following vertices become bends, and Baca to go back to manholes. The bend type is chosen in the drawing panel.
  • Manhole labels (rBLabelAdd, rBLabelDelete) (preview): the first label — the manhole name in a circle, the ground level above the leader line and the invert level below it, with the «Daire — ISKI BACA YUS» template carried over from UrbanoLabels. Automatic updating, overlap resolution and pipe labels come later.
  • Element name templates in the network (rBNetworks) (preview): a template with # in place of the number (N#-A → N1-A) for manholes, for intermediate nodes (fittings and valves) if you want them separate, for pipes, and for new lines in rBHat. A line name you type by hand is now protected.
  • Drawing options in the drawing panel (preview): the drawing direction with or against the flow, the minimum pipe length, and the snap distance — taken from Urbano’s «Şebeke çiz» window, and kept for you between sessions.
  • Invert levels, slope and depth in the properties panel and the network table, edited in place; and Tutorial 4 — Lines and invert levels. Phase 2 is complete.
  • Pipe invert levels (rBInvert): along a route, as a line between two values, from a slope, or at a fixed depth or an offset below a level.
  • The user guide in Turkish is complete: every Arabic page has its counterpart in tr/.
  • The ribbon panel Tablo ve Hatlar is now named “Lines” instead of “Branches”, and so is the table tab.
  • Lines (rBHat) replace branch naming: they are derived automatically or added from manhole to manhole, and they tell you when they need updating. Branch and line
  • Tutorials 2 — Converting an existing drawing and 3 — Ground levels, the levels and roles concept, and the pages for conversion, the check, selection and levels.
  • The “delete pipes” and “delete manholes” tooltips in the ribbon now describe what the two commands actually do: deleting a manhole takes its pipes with it, and the question about the manholes left behind comes after the deletion.
  • Edit command pages: rBMove, rBMerge, rBDelete, rBReconnect, rBReverse, rBJoin, rBRenumber, rBProperties.
  • Titles that showed their internal names have been corrected: the drawing panel in the ribbon is now titled Çizim, and the settings window UrBIM 3D — Ayarlar.
  • The user guide has started: Getting started, the basic concepts, the first tutorial, and the pages for the define, draw and refresh commands.
