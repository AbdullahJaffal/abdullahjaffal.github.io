GUIDE/CommandRibbon · BoykesitrBProfileStyle

Longitudinal profile style

PreviewBuilt; its live test in Civil 3D is not complete yet.
Where to find itRibbon: Boykesit → Boykesit Şablonları
Command linerBProfileStyle
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

The Boykesit Şablonu window, modelled on Urbano’s «Profil tanımı»: everything that is drawn in the profile and how — the two scales, the datum level, the grid, the manhole bottom, each part with its lines and texts, the ground lines for each level, and the data rows with their title and values. Every text has its style, height, colour, alignment, horizontal and vertical offset, rotation and width factor, and every line has its colour, type, thickness and type scale.

Where to find it: Ribbon → UrBIM 3D → Boykesit → Boykesit Şablonları · type rBProfileStyle

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Type rBProfileStyle. → The window: at the top Şablon (your styles, with the current one starred ★) and Stil adı; below it on the left the Profil parçaları tree, in the middle the settings of what you select, and on the right the preview.
  2. Choose a style, or Yeni (starts from the first style) or Kopyala.
  3. Choose in the tree what you want to edit — or click it in the preview. → Its settings appear in groups, and what it draws lights up in the preview and the rest fades.
  4. Change the setting. → The preview changes at once. Click any setting to see its explanation in the box under the settings.
  5. ★ Geçerli yap to make it the style with which rBProfile draws the next profile.
  6. Kaydet. → «Şablonlar kaydedildi. Bir sonraki rBProfile geçerli (★) şablonla çizer.» — and the window stays open.

Satır başlığı under each row The format of that row’s title

Text format has nine settings everywhere: Yazı stili, Yazı yüksekliği (mm), Yazı rengi, Yatay hizalama, Düşey hizalama, Uzaklık x (mm), Uzaklık y (mm), Döndürme açısı (°), Genişlik faktörü (1 as the style draws its letters, 0.8 narrower — for a long value that its cell cannot hold). An empty box is inherited: what will be drawn shows in it in grey (from Veri formatı or Başlık formatı, otherwise as the profile draws it), so changing Veri formatı once changes every row you did not customise.

The window’s tools

Section titled “The window’s tools”
  • Satır ekle (under the chosen row), Satırı kopyala (with all its settings), ▲ ▼, Satırı sil.
  • Biçimi al then choose another node and Biçimi uygula: it takes the first one’s lines and texts, not what it shows. A row’s values can be pasted onto another row or onto its title or onto a part’s text.
  • Varsayılana döndür: the node as in the first style. A setting that differs from its default has its title in bold and beside it ↺ returns it alone.
  • Dimensions on the preview: click in an offset box of the frame’s offsets or in the title column’s width → its dimension is drawn in yellow on the preview with its value, and follows it as you type.
  • Basit görünüş: the basic settings only, for a start.
  • Ara: type part of a setting’s name (such as «yükseklik») → it appears from all the parts and rows, and you edit it from here.
  • A red box is a value that is not accepted (height 0, a colour outside 0…256…) and is not applied.

İçe aktar… and Dışa aktar…: one style in a .json file that you give to a colleague; the imported one takes an unused name and does not overwrite a style of yours.

What changes

Section titled “What changes”

Your file %AppData%\UrBIM3D\ProfileTemplates\library.json — not the drawing. Drawn profiles stay with the style they were drawn with (a copy of it is saved with them); the new style is for the next profile. And profiles drawn before this update stay “current”: nothing in them changes unless you set the new settings.

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”
  • Nothing is saved if a style has an error — an empty or duplicate name, a scale or step that is not positive, a fixed level with no value, a row with no title or with a field in the wrong place or with boxes outside 0…6, a text or line that cannot be drawn — and the message names the style and the reason.
  • Yazı stili is the name of a text style in the drawing; a style that does not exist is drawn with the current style.
  • The ruler numbers on the right are a mirror of the left: unless you set their alignment they stand after their point, and Uzaklık x (mm) is counted from the frame outwards on both sides.
  • The outer border and the automatic title column measure texts by estimate (letter width 0.7 of its height), because the actual width of the font is known only to AutoCAD; if the column does not hold a title, type its width.
  • Editing a cell in the drawing (rBProfile) shows its value without the prefix and suffix, and reads what you type divided by the factor: in a row with a factor of 100 you write the level in centimetres and it is saved in metres. And «Bileşik veri» cells cannot be edited, as they have more than one field.
  • An offset value stays the value of its manhole: to edit it in the drawing click in its cell under the manhole itself, not on the offset text. And the offset measures texts by estimate like the frame.
  • The first style does not come back if you delete it; and at least one style always remains.
  • The preview is a sample line of four manholes with the levels of your drawing, drawn with the same calculations that rBProfile draws with — so what you see is what will be drawn. And the text style does not show in the preview (it is drawn in one font), but it shows in the drawing.
