GUIDE/CommandrBMetrajKesif

Keşif schedule (Metraj Keşif)

PreviewBuilt; its live test in Civil 3D is not complete yet.
Command linerBMetrajKesif
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

Writes the quantities saved in the drawing to Excel as a Keşif schedule grouped by Poz and opens it: for each network the earthworks, backfill, manholes and pipes, with price columns. You write the prices, and Excel calculates the amounts and the totals. The schedule follows the order of Metraj Keşif Tablosu in UrbanoMetraj itself. It calculates nothing — the quantities are what rBMetraj saved.

Where to find it: the Keşif tablosu button in the Metraj tab of the workspace, or type rBMetrajKesif.

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”

  • The drawing must have saved quantities: run rBMetraj first. Otherwise the command says «Bu çizimde kayıtlı metraj yok: önce rBMetraj ile hesaplayın.».

  • The Poz numbers and the descriptions come from the catalogs, read when the schedule is written:

    • Excavation: the network’s soil — chosen in rBEarthworks, with its Poz and description in the earthworks catalog;
    • Backfill: the material of the trench layer — the materials of the earthworks catalog;
    • Pipe: its product — the pipe type’s Poz, description and class in rBCatalogs;
    • Manhole piece: its description in the assembly catalog in the drawing; its Poz is what the quantities saved, as in UrbanoMetraj.

    If you change a Poz or a description after the calculation there is no need to calculate again: the schedule writes what it is today (except the Poz of manhole pieces).

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Press Keşif tablosu (or type rBMetrajKesif). → On the command line «Metraj keşif tablosu yazıldı: {0}».
  2. The file opens in Excel: <drawing>_Metraj_Kesif.xlsx next to the drawing (and in the temporary folder for a drawing that was not saved). If a file with this name already exists it is not overwritten — it may be a schedule in which you wrote prices; the date and time are added to the name of the new file.
  3. Fill in the Proje Bilgileri sheet; then write the unit prices in the BF columns in the sheet of each network.

The sheets

Section titled “The sheets”

Proje Bilgileri — the project name, its location, the owner, the contractor… empty lines that you fill in.

A sheet for each network (with its name). At its head is «{0} — Metraj Keşif Tablosu» and the scope of the quantities and the time they were calculated; if they are stale the line is red. The rows:

Section What it contains
{0} İŞLERİ The works of the whole network; its amounts are the total of what is under it
KAZI-DOLGU İŞLERİ → KAZI İŞLERİ With the Poz and description of the network’s soil: all the excavation (m³) — and the price is written on it. If the manhole excavation is calculated, under it BACA (the manhole excavation) and BORU (the trenches) with no prices; otherwise a single row. If no soil was chosen, under the name KAZI and with no Poz
KAZI-DOLGU İŞLERİ → DOLGU İŞLERİ A group for each material, titled with the layers it is used in (for example YATAKLAMA / GÖMLEKLEME). Its first row is the material’s Poz and its total — and the price is written on it. Under it, where the quantity is: BACA or BORU, then YATAKLAMA, GÖMLEKLEME (BORU ETRAFI, BORU ÜSTÜ), GERİ DOLGU — with no prices. The manhole backfill is written under the materials of its backfill layers, and its foundation under the material of its first layer
MUAYENE BACALARI İŞLERİ With Baca Prefabrik only: a group for each manhole size («Ø{0} MM İÇ ÇAPLI BACALAR»), and in it a row for each prefabricated piece and its height — Poz, description and count (Adet). For the variable pieces («değişken») whose height is set on site: those whose height falls in one band are one row, and the band is at the end of the description: «{0} ({1:0.00}-{2:0.00} m)». The band width is in the Değişken parça yük. aralığı field in the Metraj tab. A piece with no description: «{0} {1:0.00}m»
BORU İŞLERİ Groups by class and material; and a row for each diameter: the product’s Poz and description and the total length (m). A product with no description: «Ø{0} {1} boru»

The columns and the prices

Section titled “The columns and the prices”

SIRA NO (AT.01.02…, with the first two letters of the network name) · POZ NO · MALZEME KODU (you write it) · POZ KODU · İMALAT AÇIKLAMASI · BİRİM · MİKTARI · four unit prices (MALZEME BF, İŞÇİLİK BF, GENEL GİDER BF, KAR BF) · BİRİM FİYATI · four amounts · TOPLAM TUTAR.

  • Write the price only in the rows that have price columns: the excavation row, the total row of each material, each manhole piece, and each pipe diameter. BİRİM FİYATI is the sum of the four prices, and each amount = the quantity × the price.
  • The rows under a material only say where the quantity is, and have no prices — so the material is not priced twice.
  • The amount of each row above is the sum of the amounts of the rows directly under it — so the network row is its total amount.

The grouping buttons: the buttons on the left show the network, or the sections, or a single line for each material, or all the details; the buttons above the columns fold the four prices into BİRİM FİYATI and the four amounts into TOPLAM TUTAR.

The description and the unit — the Poz book: the row’s description is the description of its own entry (the soil, the material, the pipe type, the manhole piece); if it has none, the Poz book fills it in from the Poz number. If Keşif açıklaması Poz kitabından is ticked in the Metraj tab, every row takes its number’s entry in the book and the book’s description. The unit is always the unit of the quantity; if the item is paid in another unit (the tonne, for example), the unit cell is coloured and carries a note: «Poz kitabı bu kalemi «{0}» birimiyle ödüyor (Poz {1}). Miktar bu satırın biriminde; çevrilmedi.». There is no conversion.

What changes in the drawing

Section titled “What changes in the drawing”

Nothing. Only the file is written.

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”
  • The manhole excavation and backfill (the BACA rows) follow the manhole options in the quantities: with Baca kazısı Yoksay they are not in the schedule at all, as in UrbanoMetraj. If the manhole overlap is calculated, they are written after it is deducted from each manhole.
  • MUAYENE BACALARI İŞLERİ are the same rows as in UrbanoMetraj, counted from the piece stacks that the quantities saved. A manhole with no pieces — cast in place, or with no match in the assembly catalog — is not in them, as in UrbanoMetraj; and the one with no match is counted by the Baca parçaları tab in the Metraj tab in a red row.
  • The piece’s Poz is what the quantities saved, as in UrbanoMetraj; and its description is from the assembly catalog in the drawing at the time the schedule is written.
  • With Baca Yerinde döküm this section is not there at all: no prefabricated pieces are bought. UrbanoMetraj wrote the prefabricated pieces without looking at this option.
  • A variable piece whose height falls exactly on the edge of a band is in the band it starts (0,30 m in bands of 0,10 m: 0,30-0,40). The UrbanoMetraj division sometimes put it in the band below.
  • The loose volume is for all the soil excavation — the manhole excavation included.
  • If bulking is on (Kabarmış kazıyı göster (gevşek hacim) in the Metraj tab): after the priced excavation row comes a row «Kabarmış hacim (k = {0:0.00})» — the loose volume, with no price (the soil is priced once). If the soil has no factor, the row says «Kabarmış hacim: zeminin kabarma katsayısı yok» and its quantity is empty.
  • The schedule is created anew every time: the prices you wrote in a previous schedule do not carry over to the new file (and the previous file stays as it is).
