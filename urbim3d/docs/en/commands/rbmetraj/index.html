GUIDE/CommandrBMetraj

Quantities

PreviewBuilt; its live test in Civil 3D is not complete yet.
Command linerBMetraj
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

Calculates the excavation, bedding, pipe surround and backfill quantities of the pipe trenches, for the whole drawing, for a network, or for the pipes you pick, and saves them in the drawing. The calculation is the UrbanoMetraj engine, carried over as it is. The total of each network is written on the command line, and the full numbers are in the Metraj tab of the workspace.

Where to find it: type rBMetraj (not on the ribbon yet).

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”

Every pipe needs a product, two invert levels and a trench type (rBEarthworks «Uygula» or rBTrenchType), and every manhole needs a level at Kazı Seviyesi and Dolgu Seviyesi (rBLevels). An element with something missing is left out of the calculation and named — it is not calculated with zero.

Manholes also need: a pit type for each manhole (rBEarthworks «Uygula» or rBPitType), and the assembly catalog its pieces are taken from (rBCatalogs). A manhole with no pit type still gets its pieces, but its pit is not calculated, and it is named.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. If you picked pipes or manholes before running the command, the scope is your selection: the pipes you picked, and the pipes whose two ends are both picked. Otherwise the command asks: «Metrajın kapsamı» — Tümü (the default, Enter), or Sistem, or Seçim.
    • Sistem: the network you are working on; if there is no working network and there is more than one network, it asks which.
    • Seçim: pick the pipes or manholes, then Enter.
    • Önceki: the scope of the saved quantities again — shown if the drawing has saved quantities.
  2. → «Metraj hesaplandı ve çizime kaydedildi ({0}). Kazı ve dolgu çakışması: paylaştırılır (SP).», then a line for each network: the number of pipes, the total length, the excavation, the bedding, the surround, the backfill, and the number of manholes.
  3. If elements are left out of the calculation: «{0} öğe hesaba girmedi:» and under it each one with its reason.
  4. → The Metraj tab opens in the workspace (below).

The Metraj tab

Section titled “The Metraj tab”

It shows the saved quantities, and calculates nothing itself.

  • The bar at the top tells their state: green «{0}, {1:g} — güncel.» (the scope and the time of the calculation), or yellow «{0}, {1:g} — eski: hesaplandığından beri bir kot, akar, baca yeri, hendek tipi ya da baca seçeneği değişti. Aşağıdaki rakamlar o anın rakamları; «Yeniden hesapla» aynı kapsamı yeniden hesaplar.», or «Bu çizimde kayıtlı metraj yok. «Hesapla…» ile hesaplayın; kapsamı (bütün çizim, bir sistem ya da seçim) komut satırında seçersiniz.» if the drawing has no quantities. The old quantity numbers stay visible until you calculate again.
  • Yeniden hesapla runs rBMetraj with the scope of the saved quantities; Hesapla… asks for the scope on the command line; and Yenile reads again. If an element is selected, it is released first — double-clicking a row selects its element, but the tab’s button does not mean the quantities of that element alone.
  • Excel’e aktar writes the saved quantities to an Excel file and opens it — rBMetrajExcel; and Keşif tablosu writes the Keşif schedule with the Poz numbers and the price columns — rBMetrajKesif.
  • The Sistem: list shows the rows of one network or Tüm sistemler; the four tabs and their totals follow the network you chose.
  • Kabarmış kazıyı göster (gevşek hacim) (starts off): adds the loose volume under the excavation in Özet — below.
  • Baca seçenekleri (opens and folds): the manhole calculation options of UrbanoMetraj, and the Değişken parça yük. aralığı field for the Keşif schedule — below. It shows even if the drawing has no quantities, because it decides the next calculation.
Sub-tab What it contains
Özet The number of pipes and manholes, the pipe length (and Net boru uzunluğu (m) if the manholes were calculated), the excavation, the bedding, the surround and the backfill; the manhole excavation, bedding and backfill — or «metraja girmiyor (Baca seçenekleri: Yoksay)»; what was deducted from the excavation and the backfill for overlap and in the manhole excavation; and under them «Hesaba girmeyenler: {0}», each with its reason
Borular Each pipe: the diameter, the length, Net uzunluk (m), the volumes, the deducted amounts (and Bacada düşülen kazı (m³) and Bacada düşülen dolgu (m³) if the manhole excavation is calculated), the trench type, and the network; and at the bottom Toplam
Bacalar Each manhole: Baca tipi (its name in UrbanoMetraj, such as «Baca O1000 - (1 Giris / 1 Cikis)») and Baca kazı tipi, the cover level, the depth, the excavation depth and the backfill depth; and if the manhole excavation is calculated: Kazı hacmi (m³), the backfill and Baca yataklama (m³) (and the two manhole-overlap columns if that is calculated too) and at the bottom Toplam. If the backfill level is below the invert level, the row is shown in red
Malzemeler The volume of each material by its place in the trench (bedding, around the pipe, above the pipe, backfill); if the manhole excavation is calculated, with the manhole columns Baca yataklama and Baca geri dolgu — and the total includes them
Baca parçaları The manhole piece list, «Baca BOM» in UrbanoMetraj: with Baca Prefabrik each piece with its size, height, count, Poz, description and volumes; with Yerinde döküm the number of manholes for each size and Toplam beton derinliği (m). Manholes to which the assembly catalog gave no pieces are counted in a red row («Montaj kataloğunda eşleşme yok:»). It appears if the manholes were calculated
Baca baca parçalar With Prefabrik only: the pieces of each manhole from the bottom to the top — the height, the count, the Poz and the volumes; a manhole with no pieces gets «(prefabrik eşleşme bulunamadı)» or «(yerinde döküm)». Double-clicking goes to the manhole

Double-click a row of a pipe, a manhole or an element left out of the calculation: the element is selected in the drawing and the screen goes to it. The names are the elements’ names today — so renaming does not make the quantities stale. The tab, while it is in front, refreshes after every command; if you change a level, the bar turns yellow at once.

Bulking (loose volume)

Section titled “Bulking (loose volume)”

Excavated soil swells when it leaves its place. If you choose Kabarmış kazıyı göster (gevşek hacim), the quantities show two numbers: the volume in place as calculated (Kazı (m³)), and the loose volume (Kabarmış kazı (m³)) = the excavation × the bulking factor of the network’s soil. If the network shown is a single one, its factor is written too (Kabarma katsayısı).

  • The factor is the value of Kabarma katsayısı in the Zeminler tab of rBCatalogs, for the soil chosen for the network in rBEarthworks. The program comes with no ready-made table; you enter the value.
  • A soil with no factor is not given a loose volume (and the factor is not taken as 1): «zemininin kabarma katsayısı olmayan sistem: {0}» is written in place of the value. If several networks are shown and one of them has no factor, the total is not given either — so that the total of some of them is not read as the total of all.
  • The excavation alone swells; the backfill materials stay at their measures.
  • This is a display choice: nothing is recalculated, the saved numbers do not change, and the quantities do not become stale. The choice is saved in the drawing, and rBMetrajExcel and rBMetrajKesif follow it.

Manholes

Section titled “Manholes”

After the trenches, each manhole goes through the manhole calculation of UrbanoMetraj — carried over as it is too:

  • Its pieces. The base is chosen from the connection-base table for the family of the largest pipe connected to it and from the manhole diameter; and from the family in Montaj ailesi for the manhole type alone, if there is one. Above it come the body rings, the cone and the cover, according to the manhole depth.
  • Its excavation. From its own pit type: the working margin and the batter in the tier its depth falls in. The pit is square on the outer size of the base (or the inner size with the Baca kazı genişliği option), and under the base come the foundation layers. UrbanoMetraj applied one rule to all manholes by size; here each manhole uses its own type.
  • Its backfill. From the pit up to the backfill level, the volume of the manhole itself, its foundation and the share of the trenches are subtracted; the rest is divided among the backfill layers of the pit type.
  • The net length of the pipes. The pipe length is shortened to the manhole wall at its invert level at both ends.
  • It also calculates what the pit shares with the trenches and with the neighbouring pits.

This appears in the tab (above), in the rBMetrajExcel file, in the BACA rows of the Keşif schedule rBMetrajKesif, and in the rBMetrajCompare file. The Baca kazısı option decides whether the manhole excavation enters the quantities, and whether what falls inside the manhole pit is deducted from the pipe excavation.

Manhole options

Section titled “Manhole options”

Baca seçenekleri are the manhole calculation options in Genel Ayarlar of UrbanoMetraj — in the same order and with the same defaults, and they pass the engine its own settings.

  • The options are saved in the drawing and are part of the calculation: if you change one, the saved quantities become stale — the bar turns yellow, and model health reports it. Yeniden hesapla calculates them with the new options. The quantities keep with them the options they were calculated with, and the tab and the outputs show them accordingly.
  • Hold the pointer on a line: it shows what it does.
Option Default What it does
Baca Prefabrik The piece list: the prefabricated pieces piece by piece, or Yerinde döküm — the number of manholes for each size and the total of the casting depth. It does not change the volumes
Baca kazısı Yoksay Hesapla: the manhole excavation, bedding and backfill enter the quantities, and what falls inside the manhole excavation is deducted from the excavation and backfill of each pipe (its bedding and surround do not change). Yoksay: the manhole excavation does not enter the quantities, and the trenches stay whole
Parça sayısı yöntemi Büyükten küçüğe The body rings and neck rings that fill what is left of the manhole height: from the largest to the smallest, or the closest combination without exceeding the height
Net uzunluk yöntemi Dış çap The net length of the pipe up to the outer wall of the manhole (inner radius + wall) or up to its inner face (inner radius)
Bacalar arası kazı çakışması Yoksay Hesapla: the volume shared between the pits of two neighbouring manholes is deducted from the excavation and backfill of each. It has no effect unless the manhole excavation is calculated
Baca altı beton parçası Yok Eklensin: if the assembly catalog has a piece under the manhole that suits its base, its height is added to the excavation and backfill depth
Baca kazı genişliği Dış çap The pit floor: the base size + the wall on both sides + the working margin on both sides; with İç çap with no wall
En kısa hâlinden alçak baca Prefabrik kalsın, uyarılsın If the mandatory pieces of the manhole do not fit even in their shortest arrangement: it stays prefabricated and a warning is given, or it is calculated as cast in place

With Yoksay, UrbanoMetraj wrote the manhole excavation as 0,000. The tab writes «metraja girmiyor (Baca seçenekleri: Yoksay)» instead and does not show the manhole volume columns: so that it is not read as a pit whose volume is zero.

Under the options is the Değişken parça yük. aralığı field (m; default 0,50, between 0,10 and 5,00): the Keşif schedule gathers in one row the variable manhole pieces («değişken», whose height is set on site) whose height falls in one band of this width — «Değişken Parça Yük. Aralığı» in UrbanoMetraj. Type the value then press Enter or leave the field; the comma and the point are the same. It is saved in the drawing, and only the Keşif schedule reads it: the quantities do not become stale.

And under it Keşif açıklaması Poz kitabından: if it is ticked, the Keşif schedule writes each row with its number in the Poz book and the book’s description; if it is not ticked (the default), with the entry’s own description, and the book fills in what has no description. It is also saved in the drawing and does not make the quantities stale.

Why the scope matters

Section titled “Why the scope matters”

When two trenches cross, the volume shared by the two pipes is not counted for both; it is divided between them (SP) as in UrbanoMetraj. So the calculation runs among the pipes in the scope: the trenches of different networks cross too, so the quantities of the whole drawing are not the sum of the quantities of the networks each calculated alone. And in a selection, only the overlap among the selected pipes is deducted — like «CAD Seçimi» in UrbanoMetraj.

What changes in the drawing

Section titled “What changes in the drawing”
  • The quantities are saved in the drawing — one set of quantities per drawing, and the new one replaces the previous. A single U undoes them.
  • What its manholes use from the assembly catalog (the connection bases and their families) is copied into the drawing, and the quantities are calculated with this copy: so the drawing gives the same result on another machine. A family or base in the copy stays as it is even if it later changes in the catalog.
  • The saved quantities carry a fingerprint of the data they were calculated from: if a manhole’s position or level, an invert level, a trench type, a manhole pit type, the assembly family of a manhole type, or one of the manhole options changes, they become stale: model health shows that with a Metraj row until you calculate again. Changing an element’s name or note does not make them stale.
  • The pieces of each manhole calculated are written — as the engine proposed them (they appear in Özellikler). If you set a manhole’s pieces yourself (rBManholeStack) they stay and the quantities are calculated with them; if the manhole gets deeper afterwards or the engine finds another base for it, the list of what did not enter the calculation says so. Pieces that did not change are not written again.
  • The lines and the blocks do not change.

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”
  • If it takes longer than a second, the wait window appears and says what is happening: «Çizim okunuyor» → «Hesap motoru çalışıyor» → «Tablolar hazırlanıyor». The delay and turning the window off are in rBSettings.
  • Before the calculation, if the networks to be calculated have open (not accepted) errors in rBControl, the command line gives their number («Uyarı: hesaplanacak sistemlerde {0} açık kontrol hatası var (ayrıntı: rBControl). Metraj yine de hesaplanıyor.»). This is a warning only: the take-off is always calculated.
  • If the manhole foundation has several layers, all of its volume is written under the material of its first layer — as in UrbanoMetraj: the engine calculates one volume for the foundation, not one for each layer.
  • The piece list and its calculation are from UrbanoMetraj (BuildBomBySystem, carried over as it is): it is counted from the piece stacks that the quantities saved. Quantities saved before this version are treated as stale — they did not save the manhole size.
  • And MUAYENE BACALARI İŞLERİ in the Keşif schedule in the next step.
  • If the manhole options change, the quantities are not recalculated on their own: the calculation is work you start.
  • If the assembly catalog cannot be read: «Montaj kataloğu okunamadı: {0}. Dosyanın ÜZERİNE YAZILMADI; bacalar çizimin montaj kopyasıyla hesaplandı. Ayrıntılar günlük dosyasında.», and the manholes are calculated with the copy in the drawing.
  • The ground between two manholes is linear — as in UrbanoMetraj.
  • Excel file: rBMetrajExcel writes the saved quantities; and the Keşif schedule: rBMetrajKesif. And the comparison table with UrbanoMetraj: rBMetrajCompare.
