GUIDE/CommandrBLabelAdd

Labels

TestedLive-tested in Civil 3D.
Command linerBLabelAdd
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

Puts a label at every manhole you select: a circle with its name, and a leader line from the manhole to the circle, with the ground level above it and the invert level below it. The values are read from the manhole’s own data, so they are not typed by hand.

Where to find it: type rBLabelAdd (the ribbon button and the label panel come later)

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Run the command. → «Etiketlenecek bacaları ve boruları seçin (bir etiketi seçmek sahibini seçer):». Or select the manholes before you run it.
  2. Select the manholes (by clicking or with a window) → Enter. → «{0} yeni etiket çizildi, {1} bacada aynı şablonun etiketi zaten vardı ve olduğu gibi bırakıldı, {2} etiket yeni şablonla değiştirildi.».

Selecting an existing label means selecting its manhole.

What changes in the drawing

Section titled “What changes in the drawing”
  • At every manhole, a label with the «Daire — ISKI BACA YUS» template (carried over unchanged from UrbanoLabels, designed for a scale of 1:1000): a circle of radius 5 m, 17 m below the manhole, containing the manhole’s name; a leader line that starts 1 m from the manhole and stops at the edge of the circle; above it the ground level (the «KIRMIZI KOT» level) and below it the invert level of the outgoing pipe. The angle is taken from the rotation of the manhole symbol, so a manhole rotated with its street takes its label with it.
  • The texts always read upright, whatever the direction of the line.
  • An incoming pipe at a different level: every pipe that enters the manhole with an invert level that differs from the outgoing pipe’s (by more than 5 mm) has its level written below that pipe, 5 m from the manhole, and its end is marked with a filled triangle pointing at the manhole — so you can see where the drop comes from. The triangle is compared at the outer crown, so a larger pipe entering at the same invert is marked even though its level is not written. Pipes at equal levels are not written, and a pipe with no level is written as «???». This applies to the pipes of the same network.
  • The label goes on the network’s label layer (<prefix>_ETIKET), which is created if it does not exist.
  • The template is saved in the drawing, so the labels can be rebuilt for anyone who opens the file.

The label follows the manhole

Section titled “The label follows the manhole”
  • Every one of our commands ends by updating the labels, and so do edits from the Properties panel and the table: change an invert level with rBInvert or move a manhole with rBMove, and the label has already changed before the command ends, in the same undo step. The message «Etiketler güncellendi: {0} yeniden çizildi, {1} sahipsiz etiket silindi.» appears only when something changed.
  • A moved manhole takes its label with it, by the same offset; and a label you dragged by hand stays where you put it. Dragging the circle alone with MOVE brings the line and the texts after it at the next of our commands.
  • Deleting the manhole takes its label with it.
  • Ordinary AutoCAD edits (MOVE on the manhole symbol, for example) are not intercepted: the label catches up at the next of our commands, with rBLabelUpdate, or at the end of rBRefresh.

Pipe labels

Section titled “Pipe labels”

Select a pipe (or its label), together with manholes or on its own, and it gets a label with the «Boru — Uzunluk, Eğim / Çap, Aile» template: length and slope above the pipe, diameter and family below it, at its midpoint, parallel to it and readable. The slope uses the format from the settings.

  • Manhole labels are placed first, then pipe labels around them.
  • If the midpoint position is taken, the label slides along the pipe, then moves to the other side, then leaves the pipe with a leader line as a last resort.
  • A pipe shorter than its text gets its label outside it, with a leader line to it.
  • The label follows the pipe: it stays at the same fraction of its length wherever the pipe is moved or stretched, it updates when the pipe’s length, slope or type changes, and it is erased when the pipe is deleted.
  • «{0} yeni boru etiketi çizildi, {1} boruda aynı şablonun etiketi zaten vardı ve olduğu gibi bırakıldı, {2} etiket yeni şablonla değiştirildi.»

Line and branch labels

Section titled “Line and branch labels”

If you selected nothing before the command, it first asks: «Ne etiketlensin» — Eleman (manholes and pipes, the default), or Hat, or Kol. Choose «Hat» or «Kol», then select pipes → «Etiketlenecek hatların ya da kolların borularını seçin:» → one label is placed for each line (or branch) that the selected pipes belong to:

  • Line: its name above and its length below. Branch: «from–to» (its first and last manhole in the direction of flow) above and its length below.
  • At the midpoint of the path’s length, on the pipe where the midpoint falls, like a pipe label. The template can also place it from the start or the end, on every pipe, or repeated at an interval.
  • It is arranged after the manhole and pipe labels: branches, then lines; it slides, flips side or leaves the pipe like a pipe label.
  • It updates when the line’s name, length or path changes, and it is erased when the line is deleted or the branch is broken (reconnect, delete, reverse).
  • «{0} yeni etiket çizildi, {1} tanesinin aynı şablonla etiketi zaten vardı ve olduğu gibi bırakıldı, {2} etiket yeni şablonla değiştirildi.»; and if the pipes are not in any line: «Seçilen borular hiçbir hatta ya da kolda değil.».

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”

  • From the label panel: the Etiketle button runs this command on what is in the Seçim list (a selection, the whole network, a line, a branch, a saved selection…) and for the kind chosen in the panel, without asking: manholes only, pipes only, lines, or branches; and the template chosen in the panel is used instead of the network’s template if it is a different one.

  • The position comes from Konum in the panel, whether you typed the command or used the button. The default is Olduğu yerde kalsın: a label from the same template stays, and a label from another template is replaced where it is. Şablondaki yerine çizilsin redraws the selection’s labels at the template’s position, and Kılavuz açısı puts the manhole labels at the chosen direction and angle.

  • Where the template comes from: the network’s template for this kind of label if it has one, otherwise the built-in template; it is read from the drawing first, then from your library in %AppData%\UrBIM3D\LabelTemplates\library.json (created with the built-in templates on first use, and what you have edited there is never overwritten). The template that is used is saved in the drawing.

  • It does not put one label on top of another: a new label avoids existing labels, manhole symbols, its manhole’s pipes and their levels, and the other new labels (in the order they were selected). If the template position is taken, it turns around its manhole — large jumps first (180°, 90°…), then by the template’s step — and then moves further out, but no further than twice its distance. If it finds no place, it stays at the template position, and the message says how many labels were moved and how many found no place: «Çakışmamak için {0} etiket döndürüldü ya da uzaklaştırıldı; {1} etikete boş yer bulunamadı ve şablonun yerinde bırakıldı.». This is always on.

  • It fills gaps and does not erase: a manhole that already has a label from the same template stays as it is, so running the command again does not move a label you moved by hand. Manholes you did not select are not touched.

  • A missing value is printed as «???», not left blank: a manhole with no ground level, or an outgoing pipe with no invert level. At the last manhole (the outfall), the lowest invert level among its pipes is written.

  • «{0} baca ya da boru çizimde yok; etiketlenmedi.»: the manhole is registered but its symbol is not in the drawing; it is left without a label.

  • Moving a label with MOVE does not increase the counter of elements that need a refresh: the label is not part of the network.

  • To untangle overlapping existing labels (placed at different times, or after manholes were moved): rBLabelClash.

  • Not available yet: choosing a different template.

  • U undoes the whole command in one step.
