GUIDE/CommandrBLabelPick

Label by clicking

PreviewBuilt; its live test in Civil 3D is not complete yet.
Command linerBLabelPick
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

Labels the manhole or pipe you click straight away, then waits for the next one. You see each label settle into place and decide what comes next. Like “İnteraktif” in Urbano.

Where to find it: in the label panel Tıklayarak etiketle · or type rBLabelPick

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. In the panel, choose what to label (Bacalar, Borular, Hatlar, Kollar), and set the template and position → Tıklayarak etiketle. → “Etiketlenecek bacaya tıklayın (bitirmek için Enter):” (depending on the kind).
  2. Click a manhole — or its symbol, or its existing label. → The label is drawn straight away.
  3. Click another one, and so on. Press Enter to finish. → “{0} etiket çizildi, {1} etiket yeniden çizildi.”.

For lines and branches, click any pipe of the line or branch: the label for that route is drawn once.

What changes in the drawing

Section titled “What changes in the drawing”

Each click does what rBLabelAdd does for a single element: the panel’s template, the Konum choice in the panel, and placement that avoids the surrounding labels and symbols.

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”
  • When typed, it labels what you click: the manhole if you click a manhole, the pipe if you click a pipe (“Etiketlenecek bacaya ya da boruya tıklayın (bitirmek için Enter):”). From the panel, only the panel’s kind.
  • “Bunun bu şablonla etiketi zaten var. Yeniden yerleştirmek için panelde «Konum»u değiştirin.”: you clicked something already labelled with this template while Olduğu yerde kalsın is selected. To move it back to the template’s position or to an angle, change the position in the panel and click again.
  • “Burada etiketlenecek bir şey yok.”: what you clicked is not a manhole, a pipe or a label of one (or, in Hatlar mode, the pipe is not in a line); the command carries on.
  • A single U reverts all the command’s labels together.

Section titled “See also”
