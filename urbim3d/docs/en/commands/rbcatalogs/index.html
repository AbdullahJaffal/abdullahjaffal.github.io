Catalog management

PreviewBuilt; its live test in Civil 3D is not complete yet.
Where to find itRibbon: Tanımlar → Kataloglar
Command linerBCatalogs
Works inCivil 3D · BricsCAD V26 · AutoCAD 2026

Here you edit the pipe and manhole types in the master catalog on your computer: families, sizes and dimensions. It also holds the earthworks catalog: fill materials, soils, trench types and manhole pit types. Only what is actually used goes into the drawing.

Where to find it: Ribbon → UrBIM 3D → Tanımlar → Kataloglar · or type rBCatalogs

When to use it

Section titled “When to use it”

To add a new pipe material or class, a size that is missing, or a manhole or fitting type your project uses. And to define the shape of the trench the pipes are buried in, the manhole pit and the backfill layers in both, with the values that the excavation and backfill take-off is calculated with. First understand the difference between the master catalog and the drawing’s copy: The catalog.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Run the command. → The Katalog Yönetimi window opens with six tabs: Borular and Düğümler, then the earthworks tabs Malzemeler, Zeminler, Hendek tipleri and Baca kazı tipleri (see “Earthworks catalog” below). At the top of the window is the name of the library you are editing and the paths of its files: the active library — and you choose another with rBLibraries.
  2. Under Aileler, pick a family, or Yeni aile ekle. → Its types appear.
  3. Pick a type or Seçili aileye yeni tip ekle, and fill in its fields (see below).
  4. Press Kaydet. → The data is checked first, in all the tabs; if there is a contradiction, saving is refused and the first problem is named. If it succeeds, it writes «Ana katalog kaydedildi: {0} boru tipi, {1} düğüm tipi.» and reminds you that open drawings are not affected. If something changed in the earthworks tabs it also writes «Kazı kataloğu kaydedildi: {0} hendek tipi, {1} baca kazı tipi.»; if nothing changed in them, their file is not written again.

Fields

Section titled “Fields”

Pipes

Section titled “Pipes”
Field Meaning
Aile adı and Malzeme The family: a material and class, such as PVC, HDPE or concrete
Ad The size’s name as written in the project (such as DN 300) — a name, not a dimension
Dış çap and İç çap The two diameters in millimetres; everything is calculated from them
Et kalınlığı The wall thickness; if it does not agree with the two diameters, a warning shows the correct value
Sınıf The pipe class as set by the manufacturer or the standard
Poz no and Açıklama The item number in the bills of quantities, and a free description

Parametreler — bu tipin değerleri: Under the sizes are the values, for the chosen size, of the parameters defined in the Parametreler tab at the Tip level and for pipes — manufacturer, class… A number and text are typed; yes / no and a list are chosen; an empty one has no value. A value that does not suit its kind has a red frame, and the catalog is not saved.

Manholes and nodes

Section titled “Manholes and nodes”
Field Meaning
Kategori Manhole, grate, fitting, valve, outfall, connection point
Yapım şekli Precast, cast in place, or a ready-made part
Kesit Circular or rectangular; with it, İç çap, or İç genişlik and İç uzunluk
Açı ° The fitting’s angle (a 45° bend, for example)
Aile dosyası A DWG file for the plan symbol and the 3D solid; if it is missing, a shape is drawn from the dimensions
Montaj ailesi In manhole types only: the family in the assembly catalog. The list shows the families of that catalog, and the name can also be typed by hand. The quantities choose the manhole’s base by the connection rules from this family only; if it is left empty, the rules alone decide
Taban In manhole types only: the type’s own base — one of the bases of its family at its size. When it is chosen, the type takes with it the depth tiers and the part limits that are in the connection rules for that base; and the manholes of this type are stacked on that base, and the connection rules do not choose afterwards. (bağlantı kuralları): the rules choose as they did

Different opening sizes are different types. The rules may give a Ø1000 manhole three bases depending on the pipe connected to it (an opening of Ø200, Ø300–400, Ø500–600). Choose the type and then Kurallardan türet: a new type is added for each base the rules give at this size — such as «Ø1000 prekast baca (et 130) / 1000 TABAN Ø200» — with its base and its depth tiers. Then give each manhole the type that suits it (rBNodeType or Özellikler), and delete the old type if it is no longer used. A manhole of a type whose base was chosen is calculated as it would have been if the rules had given it the same base.

Dizilim — derinlik kademeleri ve parça sınırları: Under the table are the tiers of the chosen type. Each line is a depth range (En az (m), En çok (m) — 0 means no upper limit), and in it the least and most of the shaft rings, cone, adjusting rings and cover to be used (most −1 means no limit, and 0 means none). The quantities take the tier nearest to the manhole’s depth. The lines are edited with + − ⧉ ▲ ▼ and saved with the type. On the right type Deneme derinliği (m): and then Önizle: the parts the engine stacks at that depth, the total of their heights, and the gap left — the same stack that the quantities would give a manhole of this type.

Ekler — bu tipin her bacasında: Under it are three tabs, the first being the accessories that every manhole of the chosen type carries — ladders, gaskets, manchons… Each line chooses an accessory from the Ekler tab and writes Miktar (and Birim is the accessory’s own unit). An accessory is named once and its quantity is more than zero, or it is not saved. A single manhole changes them for itself with rBAccessories; and what changes here reaches, when it is taken into the drawing, every manhole that did not change that accessory. They are data for now: they do not enter the quantities.

Kaplamalar — bu tipin her bacasında: The second tab: the coatings of every manhole of the chosen type, in their order — bituminous insulation on the outer face, epoxy on the inner… Each line chooses a material from the Malzemeler tab (and a new line suggests the materials of kind Kaplama / izolasyon), and Yüzey (Dış yüzey or İç yüzey), and Kalınlık (mm) or Kat — either one or both or neither; both may be left empty — and Kapsam: Tüm yükseklik, or Alttan or Üstten up to a Yükseklik (m) that you type. A coating with no material or no height is not saved. They are not AutoCAD layers. When the type is copied into a drawing the materials of its coatings come with it. A single manhole changes them for itself with rBCoatings; and what changes here reaches, when it is taken into the drawing, every manhole that did not change that coating. They are data for now: how they enter the quantities will come as a rule from you.

Parametreler — bu tipin değerleri: The third tab: the values of the parameters defined in the Parametreler tab at the Tip level and for the type’s category — as with pipes. If you change the type’s category, the list changes with it. If a parameter no longer applies to this type (its level or categories changed), its value stays with a red name: the catalog is not saved until you clear it.

Earthworks catalog

Section titled “Earthworks catalog”

A second file beside the pipe catalog, in the same folder. It starts the first time from your catalogs in UrbanoMetraj, and then becomes your file: what you delete from it stays deleted.

Every list has buttons: + adds, − deletes the selected one, ⧉ adds a copy of it, and ▲ ▼ change its order. The order matters in depth tiers and in layers.

Malzemeler

Section titled “Malzemeler”

UrBIM’s library of materials. It holds the materials you choose for the bedding, surround and backfill layers, and you can add others such as concrete and bituminous insulation. Each material has a Ad, a Tür (Dolgu, Yapısal, Kaplama / izolasyon, Diğer), a Poz no under which the layer’s volume is calculated, a Birim and a Yoğunluk (t/m³) (both may be left empty), and a Açıklama. The kind, unit and density are for information for now: nothing in the quantities is calculated with them. A material that an earthworks layer or a manhole type’s layer uses cannot be deleted: the program refuses and names the types that use it. A new material appears at once in the material lists of the trench and pit tabs, and in the Kaplamalar — bu tipin her bacasında section for manhole types.

Zeminler

Section titled “Zeminler”
Field Meaning
Ad, Kazı tipi and Poz no How excavation is named in this soil and under which item it is calculated
Kabarma katsayısı The loose volume ÷ the volume in place. It multiplies the excavation when bulking is enabled in the quantities; empty means “not given”, so that soil is not given a bulked volume

Hendek tipleri

Section titled “Hendek tipleri”

On the left is the list of types, on the right the depth tiers for the chosen type, and under them the layers of the chosen tier.

Depth tiers — each depth range has a trench shape. The first tier in which the depth falls is used, and Maks (m) = 0 means no upper limit. Two tiers that meet at one depth (0–2 then 2–4) are correct; a real overlap is refused when saving.

Field Meaning
Min (m) and Maks (m) The depth range; the Aralık column writes it in words
Gen. boşluğu (m) Added on each side: the width of the trench bottom = the pipe’s outer diameter + 2 × this value
Şev (1:x) The batter of the excavation wall as in UrbanoMetraj: 1 metre to the side for every x metres of depth; 0 is a vertical wall

Layers — three lists side by side, each from the bottom to the top:

List Fields
Yataklama (alttan üste) Katman adı, Malzeme, Kalınlık (m)
Gömlekleme (alttan üste) And with it Konum (Boru etrafı or Boru üstü), and Boru üstüne kadar: a layer around the pipe that rises to its top so it needs no thickness
Geri dolgu (alttan üste) And with it Yüzeye doldur: the layer fills what remains up to the surface; only one in the list

The material cell is red until you choose a material: a layer with no material has no item.

Baca kazı tipleri

Section titled “Baca kazı tipleri”

Like the trench tab, with two differences: Çalışma boşluğu (m) is added to the manhole’s size on each side, and the layers are two lists from the top to the bottom — Alt temel (üstten alta) under the manhole’s base with a thickness in millimetres (Kalınlık (mm)), and Geri dolgu (üstten alta) around the manhole.

Assembly catalog

Section titled “Assembly catalog”

The precast manhole parts («Baca Parça Kataloğu») and the rules connecting a manhole to a pipe («Baca-Boru Bağlantı Kuralları»), in two files in the same folder: UrBIM3D.SmartAssemblyComponents.xml and UrBIM3D.SmartAssemblyRules.xml. They are in the same format as UrbanoMetraj’s «Akıllı Montaj» files: the first time, the assembly catalog you have in UrbanoMetraj is written into them as it is, and you can copy a file from UrbanoMetraj (%AppData%\UrbanoMetraj\SmartAssemblyComponents.xml and SmartAssemblyRules.xml) over them under these two names.

The manhole parts and their excavation are calculated from this catalog in rBMetraj; the quantities copy into the drawing what the manholes use from it, and calculate with that copy.

Baca parçaları

Section titled “Baca parçaları”

The tab where the families and their parts are edited. On the left are the families (Aile adı, Malzeme; + − ▲ ▼), and on the right the parts of the chosen family:

Column Meaning
Rol Base, shaft ring, cone, adjusting ring, cover, or a part under the base — the role of a new part is chosen from the Yeni parça: list and then +
Yükseklik (mm) The part’s height in the stack
Ağız Ø (mm) The upper opening of the base, the inner diameter of the ring, the lower opening of the cone, and the clear opening of the cover; and the cone’s upper opening Üst Ø (mm)
Et (mm) The wall thickness — for the pipe’s net length and for estimating the outer volume
Değişken Its height is adjusted on site; and its volume is per metre
Zorunlu One of them in every stack
Dış hacim (m³), Malzeme hacmi (m³) If left at zero, the quantities estimate them from the sizes
Poz no, Açıklama The Poz number and the description that the schedule table reads

At the bottom is Seçili parça:: the base’s shape and sizes, and Taban kalınlığı (mm) and the slab volumes; Yük sınıfı for the cover; and the length and width of the part under the base.

  • The shaft rings and the cone must fit the base’s opening, and the adjusting rings and the cover the cone’s upper opening — that is how the quantities match the parts.
  • A part or a family that a connection rule or a manhole type (Taban) uses cannot be deleted; and the bottom line says who uses it.
  • İçe aktar…: adds, from the UrbanoMetraj SmartAssemblyComponents.xml file, the families that are not in the catalog.
  • Kaydet writes the parts file alone (if something changed); it does not touch the connection rules.

Ekler

Section titled “Ekler”

The manhole accessories — ladders, gaskets, manchons… Each accessory is an independent type (like shared families in Revit): Ad, Birim (Adet, m… — and an empty one is a count), Poz no, and Açıklama. The catalog starts empty: you define the accessories.

  • The lines are edited with + − ▲ ▼.
  • An accessory that a manhole type names cannot be deleted; and the bottom line says which types name it.
  • The accessories are saved in the products catalog file with the types; and if a type is copied into a drawing its accessories come with it, and rBCatalogUpdate compares them too.
  • They are data for now: where they sit, how they are counted automatically, and their quantity take-off — later.

Parametreler

Section titled “Parametreler”

Your own fields — like shared parameters in Revit: manufacturer, year of construction, whether a tightness test was carried out… Choose one from the list on the left or add it with +, and on the right:

Field Meaning
Ad The name that appears in Özellikler, the labels and the tables; two parameters cannot have the same name
Tür Sayı, Metin, Evet / Hayır or Liste
Birim For a number only: m, mm, adet…
Seçenekler For a list only: one choice on each line
Düzey Tip: the value is on the type, and every element of it shows it — it is written under the type. Öğe: each pipe or manhole has its own value
Kimler için Boru and the node categories — at least one
Açıklama A free description
  • A parameter to which a type gives a value cannot be deleted; and the bottom line says which types.
  • The parameters are saved in the products catalog file; and if a type is copied into a drawing the parameters of its values come with it, and rBCatalogUpdate compares them too.
  • The values of parameters at the Öğe level are written for each pipe or manhole in Özellikler.
  • They are read in labels and tables by their name: the Parametre and Boru parametresi fields in rBLabelStyle and rBTable. If you rename a parameter, change it in the labels and columns that use it.
  • The values are data: nothing is calculated with them.

Poz kitabı

Section titled “Poz kitabı”

The items book: what each Poz number is paid by — Poz no, Açıklama, Birim and Kaynak (which book and which year). A price column comes later. The Poz no cell in pipe, manhole, material, soil, manhole part and accessory types points to a number in this book.

  • The book is a file in the same folder: UrBIM3D.PozBook.json. The first time it is gathered from the numbers in your catalogs: for each number, the description of the first entry that mentions it and the unit of its quantity (m for a pipe, Adet for a manhole and a part, m³ for soil and a material, and the accessory’s own unit). After that it is yours.
  • Kataloglardan ekle: adds the numbers mentioned in the catalogs that are not in the book, and does not touch what is in it. If one number is mentioned with different descriptions, the bottom says so — such as one number given to all the manhole parts.
  • The book is not saved with an item that has no number or a number written twice; and the bottom line says why.
  • rBMetraj copies into the drawing the items that its catalogs mention; and it takes what changes afterwards in the library with rBCatalogUpdate.

With Excel

Section titled “With Excel”

To edit pipe and node types in Excel in one go — such as entering dozens of diameters from a manufacturer’s list, or correcting Poz numbers in bulk:

  1. At the bottom of the window Excel’e aktar… → a file name. → The types are written as they are in the window now: a sheet for each kind (Boru tipleri, Düğüm tipleri), a line for each type, and a column for each field and each type parameter. The Kategori, Yapım şekli and Kesit columns have a drop-down list; and the grey cells are a parameter that does not apply to the type’s category.
  2. Edit in Excel, add lines, and save.
  3. Excel’den al… → the same file. → The changed values reach their types, and new lines become new types at the end of the family they name. The bottom line says how many types changed and how many were added, and what was not taken with the reason.
  4. Review and then Kaydet: it is checked like any edit. And İptal takes nothing from it.
  • A line finds its type by the Kimlik column; a line with no id is the type with the same name in its family if there is one, and otherwise a new type. If the id belongs to a type from another library, the type is added with its id — so the same type stays in both libraries.
  • A type is never deleted: a line deleted in Excel leaves its type; deleting is in this window. And a family is not created from Excel: add it here first.
  • What is not in Excel — the symbol, the base, the depth rows, the accessories and the coatings — stays in the type as it is.
  • What is not taken: an unknown column, an invalid id, a second line for the same type, a family that does not exist, a number that is not a number, a choice that is not one of the choices, and a value that does not suit its parameter or a parameter that does not apply to the type’s category. In the case of a number or choice error the type’s old value stays.
  • An empty number cell: in pipe sizes it keeps the old value, and in the optional manhole sizes and in parameters it clears the value.

What changes in the drawing

Section titled “What changes in the drawing”

Nothing. The master catalog is a file on your computer (or on the network). Each drawing keeps the sizes it was designed with; a change here reaches an existing drawing only when that type is copied into it again.

Notes and limits

Section titled “Notes and limits”
  • Several families or diameters can be selected (Ctrl/Shift) and removed together with «−».
  • Deleting a family deletes all its types from the master catalog — not from the drawings that use them.
  • If the file cannot be read, it is not overwritten; the program says so and points you to the error log. If only the earthworks file cannot be read, the window opens with the earthworks tabs disabled, and pipes and manholes work.
  • Types are given to pipes and manholes by each network’s rules in rBEarthworks, and to particular elements in rBTrenchType and rBPitType; and the quantities are in rBMetraj.
  • If the assembly catalog cannot be read, it is not overwritten, and the Montaj ailesi list stays empty and the name is typed by hand.
  • If the items book cannot be read, it is not overwritten, and its tab stays disabled.
  • Stepped excavation and shoring are not available yet, so their cells do not appear; and whatever of them is in your catalog stays saved as it is.
